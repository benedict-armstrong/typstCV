A prediction market for research papers, launched on the ICLR 2027 submissions. Researchers with an
institutional email trade each paper's outcome (Accept/Reject) with a non-convertible play
currency. Prices come from a logarithmic market scoring rule (LMSR). Since launching at the end of September 2026 it
has had about 54,000 unique visitors from 96 countries and over 600,000 page views.
