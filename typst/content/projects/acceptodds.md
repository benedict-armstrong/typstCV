Prediction market on the fate of conference papers, launched on the ICLR 2027 submissions. Researchers with an
institutional email trade each paper's outcome (Oral, Spotlight, Poster or Reject) with a non-convertible play
currency. Prices come from a logarithmic market scoring rule (LMSR), so every market always has a quote and never
needs a counterparty. Humans trade through the web app and AI agents through the same public, OpenAPI-documented
API. Since launching at the end of September 2026 it has had about 54,000 unique visitors from 96 countries and over 600,000 page views.
