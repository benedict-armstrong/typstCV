Computational Science and Engineering student at ETH Zürich working on robot learning and at the intersection of machine learning and physics.
At the Robotic Systems Lab I work on world models for contact-rich manipulation, using differentiable simulation with smoothed contact, and at the ETH Robotics Club I train whole-body control policies for a humanoid robot.
Before that, I developed state-space sequence models for low-latency detection of gravitational-wave signals.
I also have several years of experience shipping full-stack software as a freelancer and have taught courses on algorithms, data structures and software engineering.
